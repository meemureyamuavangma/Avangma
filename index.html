ABOUT

Rooted in Meemure
since 2016.

Local knowledge, responsible tourism and memorable nature experiences.

Established on 22 June 2016, Meemure Yamu with AVANGMA has been creating unforgettable nature experiences for travellers seeking the true beauty of Meemure.

We specialise in authentic camping adventures, guided jungle trekking, waterfall tours, river bathing, natural fish therapy and traditional village hospitality.

Our packages are designed for couples, families, groups, corporate teams and solo travellers who want to explore one of Sri Lanka’s most breathtaking destinations.

Our mission is to promote responsible and sustainable tourism while supporting the local community and preserving Meemure’s unique natural environment.

TOUR POLICY

Clear expectations.
Safer journeys.

Please read these conditions before confirming a reservation.

01

Booking

Advance booking is required. A booking is confirmed only after the required advance payment is received. The balance must be paid before or upon arrival.

02

Cancellation and refund

Cancellations made seven days or more before the tour may be eligible for rescheduling. Advance payments are generally non-refundable unless the tour is cancelled by Meemure Yamu. Severe weather or safety concerns may allow rescheduling.

03

Arrival and departure

Guests should arrive at the agreed meeting point on time. Delays may affect the tour schedule and activities.

04

Environmental responsibility

Do not litter or damage the environment. Respect wildlife, rivers, forests and village property. Carry plastic waste back or use designated bins.

05

Alcohol and smoking

Smoking is allowed only in designated areas. Excessive alcohol consumption, illegal drugs and disruptive behaviour are prohibited.

06

Safety

Follow guide instructions during outdoor activities. Guests participate in activities at their own risk and must disclose medical conditions, allergies, dietary requirements or mobility limitations in advance.

07

Accommodation

Respect fellow guests and maintain a peaceful environment. Intentional property or equipment damage may be charged to the responsible guest.

08

Pets

Pets are allowed only with prior approval.

09

Photography

Photos and videos may be taken for promotional purposes. Inform us in advance if you do not wish to appear in promotional content.

10

Weather and itinerary

Itineraries may change due to weather, road conditions, river conditions or unforeseen circumstances. Guest safety takes priority.

11

Local culture

Dress respectfully, be courteous to residents and help preserve Meemure’s culture and heritage.

By confirming a reservation, the guest acknowledges the applicable booking and tour policies. Final legal wording should be reviewed by a qualified Sri Lankan legal professional.
